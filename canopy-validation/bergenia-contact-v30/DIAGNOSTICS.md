# Remaining contact diagnostics

These are measurements of temporary probes, not additional production changes.
They do not establish physiological confinement or authorize weaker clearance.

## Native mature scape

A selected v30 NativeAOT day-240 checkpoint has 52 above-ground organs. Its first
scape is 5.181651 mm long and 3.6753511 mm in radius; the accepted starting geometry
is clear under existing policy. A requested 1 mm elongation / 0.25 mm thickening
fails in the native mature fixture, restoring the original state exactly.

After the first reaction, certification repeatedly fails between **petioles 5 and 28**,
sharing crown support 1. Their endpoint is clear. Twenty-six attempts retain no growth.
The generic continuation trace labels this motionCertificate; the diagnostic copy
additionally records the CCD outcome as Intersecting, rather than budget exhaustion.

The first whole-interval audit reports −9.999844 µm initial clearance and
+9.996584 µm final clearance. Sixty-five uniform samples see no gap below the initial
one. That sampling is insufficient to certify a complete path.

Recursive subdivision exposes a much smaller interval:

| Measurement | Recorded value |
| --- | --- |
| Initial clipped-proxy clearance | −9.999902 µm |
| Final clipped-proxy clearance | −9.999902 µm |
| Midpoint clipped-proxy clearance | −10.000542 µm |
| Existing accepted tolerance boundary | −10 µm |
| Combined midpoint motion bound | 0.0037462171 µm (about 3.75 nm) |

The midpoint decision is roughly **0.54 nm** past the tolerance. Rotation components
and rod endpoints move by a few floating-point units during interpolation. This is
a concrete precision-scale lead. It does not prove that the exact geometric path
is clear: an independent double-precision clipped-segment and interpolation
reference remains necessary. The current calculation lacks outward-rounded
interval arithmetic, so its Intersecting result is not evidence of physical
confinement at this scale.

The selected managed reader of the same native checkpoint retains approximately
97.656 µm elongation and 250 µm thickening instead. Those results cannot be pooled:
runtime floating-point and preceding query histories differ. Compare controls
within the same runtime; do not treat cross-runtime imports as deterministic continuation.

A task-local reciprocal-constraint experiment retains about 1.22 µm elongation
and full 250 µm thickening in its managed mature fixture. Through day 275 it still
fails to recover flowering or developed-leaf density. It is not enabled.

Recommended next acceptance fixture: replay this native state, evaluate clipped rod
distances and quaternion trajectories against double precision, preserve exact
endpoints and frame copies, and distinguish uncertain predicates from obstruction.
Any precision correction must retain the 11 µm invalid-start refusals, attachment
erosion, swept crossings, rollback, native checkpoint determinism and matched-cost gates.
A blanket tiny-motion acceptance or enlarged physical tolerance is not justified.

## V1 deformation measured in the seasonal case

The explicit-mode-1 NativeAOT state at day 275 contains sixteen whole leaves.
Accepted internal side folds reach about **4.6875 degrees**. Internal longitudinal
midrib controls remain below **0.000235 degrees**. Petiole internal controls reach
about **7.86 degrees** in one plane, with nonzero response in the other planes.
Median whole-base bends are about **25 degrees for blades** and **8.53 degrees
for petioles**. These are model results, not measured Bergenia stiffness values.

The strain restriction and coarse four-coordinate map are plausible limitations,
but these observations do not isolate causality. Substantial feasible shell bending
inside the agreed strain bound still requires the V2 shell/refinement fixtures.
Increasing microscopic leaf count or loosening growth coefficients would not pass
the biological objective.
